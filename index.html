HOW IT WORKS

No servers. No logins. Just pass the link.

Designed so anyone in the car or group chat can use it in seconds—even if they aren't tech-savvy.

1

Pick Your Modules

Start a trip in 20 seconds. Toggle on only what your crew needs: Trip Locations, Expense Splitter, Shared Playlist, or Crew Notes. Add an optional password to encrypt the URL.

2

Log Stops, Tabs & Location

Anyone on the trip can tap their name, drop a gas or hotel receipt, add a coffee stop along the route, or snapshot the car's current GPS location.

3

Tap “Share the Update”

Hit the big Share the Update button to beam the updated link straight to iMessage, Signal, or WhatsApp. Opening a newer link automatically merges everyone's additions.

Let Your AI Update the Trip Link

AI assistants don't need to compress binary hashes. Any AI can read /llms.txt and append &ai=<URL-encoded JSON> to your existing link—or build a brand-new trip at #ai=<JSON>.

02 • TURING-COMPLETE DB-SCRIPT & URL MICRO-MODULES

Programmable Micro-Modules in the Link

Every micro-module is powered by DB-Script—a sandboxed, Turing-complete mini-language (state, let, set, if/else, while, for, fn, plus UI primitives big, countdown, kv, check, btn, input, pin, progress) whose entire structure & state travel inside the URL:

  • ⏳ Countdown & Past Elapsed (countdown): Years, months, days, hours & minutes for both future countdowns and past milestones.
  • 🔑 Pinned Codes (kv) & 🅿️ GPS Pins (pin): Wi-Fi/door codes with 1-tap copy, plus dropped parking & meetup pins with walking directions.
  • 📦 Checklists, 🍕 Polls & 🧮 Custom Scripts: Tap </> Script on any micro-module to view or edit its live DB-Script code!
state target = "2026-12-25T11:11"
let t = timeDiff(state.target)
big t.text sub=t.formattedDate badge=t.badge
03 • SMART MERGE & MULTI-BACKUP VAULT

Zero Accidental Data Loss

When you open a link from the group chat, Dangerous.Business compares every item on your device against the incoming link with collapsible diffs (showing what's only on your device, what's new in the link, and what's shared). Every merge automatically saves a timestamped snapshot in ⚙ Settings → Backups.
